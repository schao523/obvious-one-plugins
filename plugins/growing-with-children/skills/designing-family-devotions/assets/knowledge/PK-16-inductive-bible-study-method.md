# PK-16 — Inductive Bible Study Method

## Purpose
Provide the professional methodology that governs IM-06 when the user explicitly asks for formal inductive Bible study or a step-by-step study of a supplied passage.

## Source basis
- RM-03: 《歸納釋經法》
- Source role: Bible-study Methodology Authority
- Use mode: substantially transformed professional method
- Original PDF required at runtime: No

## Methodological frame
The source combines inductive Bible study with hermeneutical principles and organizes study around three broad movements:

1. Observation
2. Interpretation
3. Application

The method itself is treated as a tool rather than a master; the workflow should therefore guide the user without becoming mechanically rigid.

## Ten-step method

### Step 1 — 細察事實 / Observe the facts
Identify textual facts such as genre, people, time, place, setting, authorial comments, emphasis, quotations, questions, commands, promises, warnings, illustrations, theological concepts, key words, figurative language, and difficult terms.

### Step 2 — 認清關係 / Identify relationships
Examine context and relationships within the passage, including contrasts, comparisons, repetition, grammatical structure, significant connectors, progression of ideas, translation comparison, qualitative/quantitative relationships, spatial and chronological relationships.

### Step 3 — 注意結構 / Observe structure
Determine the passage's visible structure and major divisions from the text itself, using context, connectors, shifts, topic sentences, plot movement, and major units.

### Step 4 — 勤發問題 / Form questions
Generate questions that open the text for interpretation. The source groups questions broadly into:
- definitional / explanatory;
- logical / relational;
- inferential / exploratory.

### Step 5 — 逐題解答 / Answer the questions
Answer each question carefully using appropriate interpretive principles rather than speculation. The source emphasizes answering the actual question and using established hermeneutical reasoning.

### Step 6 — 歸納總意 / Synthesize the meaning
Bring the individual answers together into a coherent whole, identify the relationships between paragraphs/units, and express the passage's overall meaning in concise form. Rewriting the meaning in one's own words is allowed, but this is not a new translation.

### Step 7 — 找出主題 / Identify the theme
State the passage's central theological proposition as a complete sentence. The theme should reflect authorial purpose and the central idea of the passage rather than merely repeat historical details.

### Step 8 — 寫下原則 / Derive the principle
Bridge interpretation and application by identifying a theological principle that:
- grows out of the interpreted meaning;
- is tested against cultural/semantic limitations;
- is consistent with broader biblical theology;
- has enduring relevance.

### Step 9 — 列出細節 / Specify application
Translate the principle into concrete application. The source distinguishes universal principle from situation-specific detail and recommends application that is specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-related.

### Step 10 — 身體力行 / Put into practice
Move from application idea to actual action, with timely execution, reminders, self-review, prayer, and appropriate accountability.

## IM-06 operating modes

### Mode A — Simple Bible guidance
Trigger:
- "What does the Bible say about...?"
- "Explain this verse."
- "Give me relevant verses."

Behavior:
- Do **not** force the full ten-step workflow.
- Retrieve/consult RM-04A/B for the actual biblical text.
- Use only as much of PK-16 as needed for sound explanation.
- Distinguish direct textual observation from supplemental model knowledge.

### Mode B — Formal inductive Bible study
Trigger:
- user explicitly asks for inductive study;
- user asks to be led step by step through a passage;
- user invokes the ten-step method.

Behavior:
- PK-16 consultation is **required**.
- RM-04A/B consultation is **required** for the passage text.
- Move in sequence through Steps 1–10.
- At interactive stages, ask a focused question and wait.
- Do not jump to application before sufficient observation/interpretation.
- Allow pause, resume, revise, skip only where skipping does not break the method's integrity.

## Interaction rules
- Prefer user observation before supplying the answer when in formal study mode.
- Ask 1–3 focused questions per step at most.
- Wait after the question set.
- If user says "just explain," switch to direct explanatory mode rather than forcing the study workflow.
- If a step reveals uncertainty requiring historical/linguistic/cultural knowledge not contained in approved references, label that material as supplemental knowledge rather than attributing it to RM-03 or RM-04.

## Interpretation safeguards
- Observe context before isolating a phrase.
- Distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Distinguish interpretation from application.
- Avoid overgeneralization.
- Do not treat paraphrase as retranslation.
- Test application across cultural distance before deriving a universal principle.
- Keep derived principles consistent with the broader biblical text.
- Do not make the method itself the authority over Scripture.
