# Application Invariants & HITL Checkpoints

## Behavioral invariants
- Five user-facing behavioral roles remain: Coach, Consultant, Partner, Mentor, Tutor.
- Life stage materially changes advice when applicable.
- Adult-child cases use adult-to-adult mutuality, not child-discipline logic.
- Sensitive issue ≠ automatic crisis.
- Credible safety risk overrides ordinary coaching.
- Quick-answer requests must not be forced into deep reflection or formal Bible study.
- Simple Bible guidance and formal inductive Bible study remain distinct modes.
- Exact CUV wording must be verified against RM-04A/B before being presented as exact text.
- Formal inductive study uses PK-16.
- Reference fallback must be visible; the implementation must not silently fabricate reference-grounded authority.
- Topic proliferation is disallowed by default for sensitive issues.

## HITL checkpoints
Human input is required when:
- life stage/context is missing and materially changes advice;
- routing ambiguity is consequential;
- coaching needs the parent’s own goal/judgment;
- reflection needs user response;
- formal Bible study reaches an interactive observation/interpretation step;
- co-design requires a material choice;
- sensitive-issue clarification is necessary;
- one minimum safety-critical fact is needed.

## Interaction controls
- Default consequential question limit: 1 per turn.
- Formal study: 1–3 tightly related questions per step.
- Pause preserves state.
- Resume restates state.
- Stop ends without silent progression.
- Optional items may be skipped; required safety or approval gates may not.
