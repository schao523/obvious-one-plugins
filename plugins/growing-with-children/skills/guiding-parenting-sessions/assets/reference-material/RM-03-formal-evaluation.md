# RM-03 Formal Evaluation — 《歸納釋經法》

## Classification
Bible-study Methodology Authority.

## Evidence inspected
The source explicitly describes the method as combining inductive Bible study with hermeneutical principles and structuring study through observation, interpretation, and application. It presents a ten-step process and explains observation categories, textual relationships, structure, question formation, interpretive reasoning, synthesis, theme, theological principle, concrete application, and action.

## Relevance
Very high for IM-06 formal Bible-study workflow.

## Authority
Professional methodology authority for **how the Plugin conducts an inductive Bible study**.

It is not Scripture itself and does not override the biblical text.

## Behavioral impact
Required for:
- formal inductive Bible study;
- explicit ten-step study;
- step-by-step guided passage study.

Optional for:
- simple verse explanation;
- verse retrieval;
- topical Bible guidance.

## Methodological strengths
- separates observation, interpretation, and application;
- requires context and textual relationships;
- delays application until meaning has been worked through;
- uses explicit questioning;
- requires synthesis/theme before deriving principles;
- warns against careless cultural transfer;
- requires concrete application and action.

## Limitations
- The PDF does not establish itself as a complete historical, linguistic, archaeological, or systematic-theology reference.
- The method can guide interpretation but cannot supply missing external facts.
- It should not be applied mechanically when the user requests only a simple answer.
- Some terminology and OCR in the supplied PDF are imperfect; the logical method is clear enough to extract reliably.
