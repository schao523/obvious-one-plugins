## 3. RM-03 decision
RM-03 is approved as **Bible-study Methodology Authority**.

It supports the ten-step formal study flow:
1. 細察事實
2. 認清關係
3. 注意結構
4. 勤發問題
5. 逐題解答
6. 歸納總意
7. 找出主題
8. 寫下原則
9. 列出細節
10. 身體力行

Runtime preference:
- use transformed PK-16;
- do not require original RM-03 PDF in public runtime;
- rights for source-PDF redistribution remain unresolved.

## 4. RM-04A/B decision
RM-04A/B are approved as **direct runtime Scripture textual authorities**.

The owner has confirmed the specific uploaded PDFs are public-domain files. They may therefore support:
- direct runtime consultation;
- exact CUV quotation;
- passage retrieval;
- structured extraction/indexing;
- packaging;
- formal Bible-study input.

They do not become commentary or historical/cultural reference sources merely because they contain Scripture text.

## 5. IM-06 reference contract
### Simple Bible guidance
Required:
- RM-04A/B when exact text or direct biblical grounding is needed.
Optional:
- PK-16.

### Formal inductive Bible study
Required:
- RM-04A/B
- PK-16

Behavior:
- follow the ten-step method;
- ask focused questions and wait;
- do not jump directly to application;
- distinguish observation, interpretation, principle, and application;
- distinguish source-derived claims from supplemental model knowledge.

## 6. IM-07 reference contract
When a devotion/activity includes actual Scripture:
- RM-04A/B required.
PK-16 is optional unless the activity is explicitly a structured Bible-study exercise.

## 7. IM-10 sensitive-issue biblical handoff
When a sensitive issue leads into biblical study:
- PK-15 remains the relational entry framework;
- CP-01 remains the safety override;
- RM-04A/B provide Scripture text;
- PK-16 governs formal study only if the user opts into formal study;
- no sensitive topic is resolved merely by launching a Bible-study workflow before relational/safety triage.
